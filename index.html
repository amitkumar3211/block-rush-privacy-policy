Privacy Policy

Block Rush: Block Puzzle (Android) · Package com.blockrush · Last updated: 24 November 2026

Block Rush ("the app", "we") is a block puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. In short: Block Rush works fully offline, has no accounts, and does not send your data anywhere.

Summary
  • No account or sign-in. No name, email address, or other personal information is requested.
  • The app does not connect to the internet. It contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.
  • Your scores, progress, coins, and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

1. Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following data locally in its private app storage. It never leaves the device unless you back it up yourself, for example through Android's app backup to your own Google account.

DataPurpose
Best scores and number of games played (Classic and Drop modes)Showing your records and whether a run is a new best
Adventure progress (levels completed, stars earned)Unlocking the next level and showing the map
Virtual coin balance, owned themes, power-up inventory, daily reward streakThe in-game economy. Coins are earned by playing and have no real-world value
Settings (sound, music, vibration, selected theme, preferred mode, tutorial completed)Remembering your preferences

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data. You can also clear it at any time from Android Settings → Apps → Block Rush → Storage → Clear data.

2. Permissions

PermissionWhy
Vibrate (VIBRATE)Short haptic feedback when you place a block or clear a line. You can turn it off in Settings → Haptic Feedback.

The app does not request the internet permission, location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or any other permission.

3. Information we do not collect

Block Rush does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, advertising ID, or usage statistics. The app has no server of its own and makes no network requests.

4. Third-party services

The current version of Block Rush includes no third-party SDKs. It does not show ads, offer in-app purchases, or send analytics or crash reports.

If a future version adds optional features such as advertising, in-app purchases through Google Play Billing, or crash reporting, this policy will be updated before that version is released, the "Last updated" date will change, and the relevant services and their privacy policies will be listed here.

5. Children

Block Rush is a general-audience game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data and has no online features, there is nothing for a parent or guardian to request deletion of beyond uninstalling the app.

6. Data security and retention

All data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps on your device cannot read. Data is kept only for as long as the app is installed. There is no copy on any server.

7. Your choices

  • Turn sound, music, and vibration on or off in the in-app Settings.
  • Clear all game data from Android's app settings, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Exclude the app from Android backup in your device's backup settings if you do not want local data included in your own Google account backup.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version will be published at this address and the "Last updated" date at the top will be revised. Material changes, such as adding a third-party service, will also be noted in the app's store listing.
